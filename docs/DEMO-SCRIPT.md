# PocketTrail demo and field trial

## A 60–90 second recording

1. Show the planner. Choose 20 minutes, a park, Evening, Calm, and “Stay seated and avoid photos.”
2. Generate a real pack. Open “About this walk” to show the model/backend and cache status. Explain that AI selects activities and personal focus; the catalog instructions and introduction are authored.
3. Save it. If live ElevenLabs is configured, explicitly add voice while connected and show 3/3 clips saved.
4. Disconnect on a real phone, reload, and open Saved walks. Start Walk Mode and use Try chime to preview the timer cue. Listen to a downloaded instruction only if live narration was configured and saved. Keep the page active; separately test your phone with its screen locked.
5. Briefly show Window or balcony for an indoor alternative. After the outdoor session, save one real observation and download the field note.
6. Close with the actual measured preparation time, outdoor time, screen checks, model results, and sponsor spending. Include failures and limitations.

Do not describe the browser test note as an outdoor trial. Do not claim voice playback or a sponsor integration until tested with the real configured service. The in-app browser's file-download test timed out; the export content was verified separately. Confirm the actual download in your normal phone/browser before recording it.
